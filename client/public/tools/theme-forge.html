Theme forge — palette extractor

Drop a theme's banner art here. It reads the image, proposes a palette, corrects it against the accessibility rules, and writes the theme.css for you.

2 · What the art contains

Clustered in OKLCH, not HSL — HSL lightness is inconsistent across hues and will confidently hand you a "dark" violet that is visually glaring.

3 · The corrected palette

Extraction donates hue and chroma only. Surface lightness is forced to the fixed ladder so art always separates from content, and text is solved for contrast rather than sampled — a colour taken from artwork has no idea what it will sit on.
