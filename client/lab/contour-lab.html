Contour lab

Measures how far detectCardContour's quad sits from a card whose true corners are known, because they were drawn. One number per attempt (otsu, otsu-inverted, edges): the mean outward offset of the four corners, in pixels and as a fraction of card width. Positive is overshoot — the quad is larger than the card.

This measures the detector's pixel arithmetic, not whether detection works. A synthetic card is exactly the clean test data that flattered the detector this one replaced — see the header of cardContour.js. Use it to ask "does this attempt sit N pixels outside the border it found, and does a change move N", never to tune thresholds or the framing ladder. Those need a recorded session and scripts/scan-replay.mjs.
