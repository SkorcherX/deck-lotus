Bulk Add Cards

Paste a list of cards to add to your inventory — the same formats the deck importer takes, so an export pastes into either. By name: "4 Lightning Bolt". With a set: "1 Abundant Growth (ECC) 97" or "4 Lightning Bolt [M21]". By printing alone: "4 DSK 123" (set code and collector number, no card name needed). Add *F* for foils. Use Preview to check what each line resolves to before adding. To load another app's export file instead, pick it under "Import from".

Generate a Deck

Builds a deck out of cards you already own. Everything here is worked out from card text, so treat it as a starting point — each card says why it was picked, and anything your collection could not cover is listed rather than hidden. Nothing is saved until you accept it.

1

Start from

A blank page, or a deck you have already built. Revising keeps the deck exactly as it is — it proposes a new list, and says what it would change.

The deck's own colours are always included. Tick another to splash it in.

Tick the cards this deck is built around. They stay in the revision whatever the rankings think, and the rest of the deck is chosen to go with them.

2

Format

What kind of deck this is. It sets the size, the land count and which cards are legal.

3

Commander

The legendary creature the deck is built around. It fixes which colours you may play and starts every game available to you, so it matters more than any other single card.

3

Colours

Which colours the deck may play. Fewer colours means more reliable mana; more colours means a wider choice of cards.

4

Build around

The thing the deck is trying to do. Every option below was measured against the cards you own in these colours, so what is offered is what your collection can actually support. Pick a second one to mix in a smaller share of it — milling your opponent beside a graveyard plan, say.

Describe your own theme

For a plan the themes above do not cover. Type words from the cards' rules text; commas separate alternatives, and "mill" also finds "mills" and "milled". Card types work too, like "Equipment".

Export Collection

Bulk Remove Cards

Paste a list of cards to take out of your inventory — the undo for an add that went in wrong. Same formats as Bulk Add: "4 Lightning Bolt", "1 Abundant Growth (ECC) 97", "4 DSK 123", *F* for foils. A line without a set matches a printing you actually own. Use Preview to see what each line would remove; nothing is removed until you confirm.
